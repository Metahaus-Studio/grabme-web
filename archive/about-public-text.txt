ABOUT GRABME

An electric mobility ecosystem built as one product.

GRABME connects the passenger journey, driver operations, business programs, Mission Control, and the in-car experience through one EV-first mobility platform.

WHAT GRABME IS

More than an e-taxi booking app.

The public passenger experience is only one layer. GRABME is designed as a connected operating system for electric mobility - riders, drivers, operations, business programs, and the cabin working together.

Electric by default

GRABME is built around an EV-only fleet rather than treating electric vehicles as a secondary ride category.

One passenger journey

Booking, matching, pickup, live ride, completion, ratings, programs, and airport mobility stay connected.

Built for operators

Mission Control gives the mobility business an administrative layer behind the passenger and driver apps.

Connected programs

Corporate, student, membership, and airport experiences reuse the same platform foundations.

Live mobility

Route, ETA, driver, vehicle, and ride state remain central to the active journey.

Connected cabin

GRABME Connect continues the authenticated ride inside supported vehicles with live trip information and cabin experiences.

PRODUCT FAMILY

Ride. Drive. Operate. Connect.

Ride gives passengers the end-to-end mobility experience.

Drive gives drivers the tools to receive and execute rides.

Operate gives GRABME the administrative and dispatch foundation behind the network.

Connect extends the active journey into supported vehicle cabins.

Electric mobility, connected. Built for passengers, drivers, businesses, and the journey inside the vehicle.
