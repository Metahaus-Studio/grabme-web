PASSENGER FLOW

One continuous ride lifecycle.

STEP 1

Choose your destination

Search a destination and review the journey from your current pickup context.

STEP 2

Choose how to travel

Select Standard, Priority, Premium, Luxury, Airport, or an eligible program journey.

STEP 3

Ride now or schedule

Request immediately or choose a scheduled ride when you want to travel later.

STEP 4

Confirm pickup & payment

Review pickup and pay with a supported passenger payment method.

STEP 5

Follow the live trip

See driver, vehicle, ETA, route, trip progress, completion, and rating.
